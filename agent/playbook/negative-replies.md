# Handling negative replies

## Respond to the concern first
When a reply is negative, don't push the next step. Acknowledge what they said in one sentence, and ask one question that finds the real concern, such as whether the problem is timing, budget or fit.

## Move it to a conversation
Repeated negative replies are hard to fix by email. Offer a ten minute call focused only on their concern, with no demo and no slides.

## Bring in another voice
If the tone stays negative, ask a manager or a technical colleague to join. A new person can reset the conversation, and it shows the buyer you took the concern seriously.
