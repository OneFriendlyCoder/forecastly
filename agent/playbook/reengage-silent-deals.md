# Re-engaging silent deals

## When a buyer stops replying
Treat two weeks of silence on an active deal as a signal to change approach, not to send the same follow-up again. Send one short message that asks a yes or no question, such as whether the project is still a priority this quarter. Make it easy to answer in one line.

## Going around the silence
If your main contact stays quiet after two attempts, reach a second person on the buying team. Ask a colleague who knows them to make the introduction if you can. Give them a reason to reply that helps them, such as a finding from similar customers, and not a request for an update.

## Knowing when to stop
If three different attempts over a month get no reply, tell the buyer you will pause and leave the door open. This often prompts an honest answer, and it keeps your forecast accurate.
