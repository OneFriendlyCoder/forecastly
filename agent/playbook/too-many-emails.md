# Fixing one-way outreach

## When you are sending and they are not replying
High email volume with few replies usually means the sequence has stopped working. Pause automated steps for this deal. More messages at this point can reduce trust.

## Change the channel
Replace the next planned email with a phone call or a message through a mutual contact. A short voicemail that references something specific to their project works better than another template.

## Make each message earn its place
Before sending again, ask what is new for the buyer. Share one relevant insight, one customer example, or one direct question. If there's nothing new, wait.
