# Recovering from missed meetings

## After a no-show
Send a friendly note the same day with two specific new times. Don't guilt the buyer. A single no-show is often just a busy calendar.

## When it happens twice
Two missed meetings suggest the meeting isn't a priority. Ask directly whether the topic is still worth their time, and offer a shorter call with a clear purpose.

## Confirm beforehand
Send a one line agenda the day before each meeting, with one question they can think about. It lowers no-shows and shows you have prepared.
