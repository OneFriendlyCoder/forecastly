# When buyers reply slowly

## Reading slow replies
Slower replies than earlier in the deal often mean attention has moved elsewhere. Compare the current pace with how quickly they replied at the start.

## Make replying easier
Shorten your messages and ask for one thing at a time. Offer options to choose between, so the buyer doesn't have to write much.

## Check the timeline
Ask if anything has changed on their side, such as a new priority or a reorganisation. Then adjust your plan to match their real timing and don't keep pressing to your own.
