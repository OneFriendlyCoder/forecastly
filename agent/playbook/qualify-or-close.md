# Deciding whether to keep a deal open

## Test the deal honestly
Ask whether there's a real problem, a budget, a decision maker you've spoken with, and a date that matters. If two or more are missing, the deal needs requalifying.

## Have the direct conversation
Tell the buyer what you are seeing and ask if it's still worth both sides' time. Buyers respect the question, and a clear no is more useful than a slow maybe.

## Close it cleanly
If the answer is no, close the deal and record why. Set a reminder to return when their situation changes.
