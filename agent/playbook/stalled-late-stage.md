# Unblocking late-stage deals

## Find the blocker
When a long-running deal stops moving, ask your champion what has changed and what would stop it being signed this month. Common blockers are legal review, procurement and a new competing priority.

## Offer to do the work
Offer to send security documents, a draft order form, or answers for procurement. Reducing their effort often restarts the process.

## Create a reason to act
Tie the decision to something that matters to the buyer, such as a project start date or the end of a current contract. Don't invent false deadlines.
