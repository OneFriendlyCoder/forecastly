# Multithreading an account

## Why one contact is a risk
A deal that depends on one person stalls when that person is busy, moves role, or loses interest. Aim to have at least three people engaged before the pricing stage, including someone who controls budget.

## How to add contacts
Ask your champion who else will be affected by the decision. Offer a specific reason to bring them in, such as a security review or a short demo for their team. Add the new contact to the next meeting and don't start a separate thread behind your champion's back.

## Reaching the economic buyer
Ask your champion what the budget owner needs to see to approve this. Offer to prepare a one-page summary of value and cost that the champion can forward.
