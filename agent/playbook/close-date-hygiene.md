# Close date hygiene

## When the date keeps moving
A close date that has moved more than once is a warning sign. Don't move it again without new evidence from the buyer. Ask what has to happen, and who has to approve it, before they can sign.

## Build a shared plan
Agree a short written plan with the buyer listing each remaining step, its owner and its date. If they won't commit to the steps, the date isn't real.

## Keep the forecast honest
If the buyer can't name a reason for the current date, move the deal to a later period and flag it to your manager, so nobody is surprised.
