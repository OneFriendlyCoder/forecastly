# Handling pricing objections

## Find out what the objection means
Ask whether the issue is the total price, the budget timing, or comparing you with another option. Each one needs a different answer.

## Return to value
Remind the buyer of the problem they described, and estimate its cost to them. Compare the price with that cost, using their numbers and not yours.

## Trade, don't discount
If you offer a concession, ask for something in return, such as a longer term or a reference. Giving a discount with nothing in return makes the original price look unreal.
